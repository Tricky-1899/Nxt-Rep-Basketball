Privacy

PRIVACY
NOTICE.

What we collect through this website, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

Nxt Rep Basketball is a basketball coaching business run by Yianni, a sole trader based in Sydney, NSW. You can contact us about anything on this page at info@nxtrepbasketball.com.au.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through our forms:

  • Free assessment form: parent or guardian name, mobile, email, suburb, the player's first name, age and current level, what you're interested in, any notes you add, and whether you'd like updates from us.
  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional), what you're enquiring about and your message.

Information about a child is provided by their parent or guardian. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to fill in the form for you.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange your child's assessment and run any sessions you book.
  • To plan coaching that suits the player's age and level.
  • To send updates about sessions and programs by email or SMS, only if you ticked the box saying you're happy to receive them. Every message tells you how to opt out, or you can email us at any time.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who else handles it

To run this website and get your message to us, we use a small number of service providers:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our email inbox.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, so your browser connects to Google when pages load.

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Australia.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to coach your child, stay in touch if you've asked us to, and meet any legal or record-keeping obligations. After that we delete them.

Your choices

  • Ask to see the information we hold about you or your child.
  • Ask us to correct it or delete it.
  • Stop marketing messages at any time.

Email info@nxtrepbasketball.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days.

Concerns

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email us first and we'll work to fix it. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
